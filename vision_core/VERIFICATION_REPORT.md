# CV Capability Verification Report

## Decision

**Software implementation: PASS with fail-closed release gates.**

**Professional-grade empirical validation: BLOCKED.** The repository contains no permission-cleared, ground-truth multi-sport athlete corpus. Synthetic media and unit fixtures are not accuracy evidence. Customer release remains disabled until each exact capability/model/protocol/metric satisfies `src/lib/capability-validation.ts`, `src/lib/drill-protocols.ts`, and `docs/professional-cv-validation-plan.md`.

## Professional validation still required

For each sport, drill, camera setup, model version, and metric:

1. Collect permission-cleared representative athlete footage across supported devices, lighting, demographics, environments, camera angles, occlusions, bystanders, and invalid/incomplete executions.
2. Synchronize timing gates, radar, optical tracking, instrumented targets/bats/balls, or motion capture as required by the protocol.
3. Independently annotate object boxes/tracks, athlete identities, attempts, invalidity reasons, and outcomes.
4. Report precision/recall, IDF1/HOTA and identity switches, segmentation precision/recall/F1, invalid-attempt sensitivity/specificity, failed-analysis rate, P90 metric error, confidence calibration error, and expert agreement.
5. Meet every threshold in `src/lib/drill-protocols.ts` with independently reviewed evidence, HTTPS evidence URI, SHA-256, exact model version, and exact protocol version.
6. Keep customer metrics, rankings, coaching, and performance claims disabled for any failed or missing gate.

## Known blockers

- No permission-cleared athlete validation corpus exists in this repository.
- No radar/timing-gate/motion-capture or expert-rubric result set exists here.
- The trained ReID path is deployed and benchmark-capable, but no permission-cleared Athlemetry corpus has measured IDF1, identity switches, or long-occlusion recovery across supported conditions.
- YoutuReID's ONNX artifact is published under Apache-2.0, but its reported training/benchmark lineage includes research-use pedestrian datasets; professional commercial deployment requires written legal approval of that chain or replacement with a consented Athlemetry-trained model.
- Instantaneous soccer acceleration is deliberately withheld: a scalar course-distance calibration cannot scientifically convert normalized pose velocity into a per-frame metric acceleration trajectory.
- YOLO-World is a functional baseline, not proof of professional per-class sports-object accuracy.
- Validation submission and approval are administrator-controlled and require two distinct administrators, but externally hosted evidence still needs independently auditable issuer/signature controls in the study process.

No professional-grade accuracy claim is authorized until these blockers are resolved with measured evidence.
