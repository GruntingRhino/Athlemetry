Athlemetry is a multi-sport video performance intelligence platform for athletes, coaches, parents, and administrators. It supports drill uploads, conservative metric extraction, cohort benchmarking, consent handling, privacy requests, and admin review. The brand should feel credible, premium, and coach-grade — more like a serious training tool than a playful consumer app or a flashy startup dashboard.

Primary users need to move quickly from sport selection to upload, then to clear feedback, trends, and benchmark comparisons. Secondary stakeholders need to trust the output, especially when the system says a clip is unclear or unsuitable for a reliable call. Parents and admins need the same design system to support consent, privacy, reports, and operational controls without making those screens feel intimidating.

Use a light, airy interface with white cards, slate text, teal/emerald primary actions, and restrained blue/amber metric accents. Prefer strong hierarchy, visible status chips, tidy dashboards, and explicit labels for units and uncertainty. Keep motion subtle and the content voice direct and honest. Avoid neon gradients, mascots, overdecorated cards, generic SaaS polish, and any language that overpromises certainty from video analysis.
