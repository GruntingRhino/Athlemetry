# Summary

- Inferred audience: athletes are the primary users; coaches, parents, and admins are secondary or external stakeholders.
- Confidence: high for athletes/coaches/admins; medium for the likely economic buyer.
- Design posture: coach-grade, premium, structured, trustworthy, and lightly expressive.
- System name: Athlemetry Performance Design System.

## Major design decisions
- Keep a light, white-card interface with strong hierarchy.
- Use teal/emerald for primary actions and trust cues.
- Reserve blue and amber for chart and warning semantics.
- Make sport context and drill guidance visible everywhere.
- Keep admin screens dense but readable.
- Treat consent, privacy, and uncertainty as first-class UI states.

## Highest-risk assumptions
- The economic buyer is likely a club, academy, school, or training organization rather than only individual users.
- The current light theme is part of the intended brand and should be preserved, not replaced with a dark dashboard aesthetic.
- Parents are important stakeholders but are not the core daily users.
